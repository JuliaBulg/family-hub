WHAT WE BUILT
--------------

1. PANTRY MODULE (fully working)

   - "Add Item" button opens a full-screen modal where you can enter:
     Item name, category, quantity, unit, expiry date
   - Items are saved to the database (Supabase) and appear in the pantry list
   - Items expiring soon show a warning banner at the top
   - Expired items show a red alert

   Fixes applied:
   - Modal scrolling was broken on Safari → fixed
   - Date picker calendar was cut off at the bottom → fixed
   - "Add to Pantry" button was hidden behind the navigation bar → fixed
   - Added always-visible scrollbar on the right side of the modal

2. IMPORT FROM RECEIPT (fully working)

   - New "🧾 From Receipt" button on the Pantry page
   - Two options:
       Photo tab — take a photo of your grocery receipt
       Paste Text tab — copy and paste receipt text manually
   - AI (Claude) reads the receipt and extracts all products automatically
   - Review screen shows all found items with checkboxes
   - You can edit item names, change categories, deselect items you don't want
   - "Add to Pantry" button saves everything selected in one go
