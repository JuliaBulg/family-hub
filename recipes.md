# 🍽️ Family Recipe Database
*Julia's family favourites — serves 4*

---

## 🍝 1. Pasta Bolognese
*Easiest & most popular!*
- 400g pasta
- 400–500g minced meat
- 1 jar tomato pasta sauce "Pinzani Napoleana" (370g)
- 5g vegetable oil
- Salt, pepper

---

## 🥩 2. Homemade Kotlet with Mashed Potato
**Kotlets:**
- 400–500g minced meat
- 1 onion
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 egg
- 50g breadcrumbs
- Grated cheese
- 5g vegetable oil
- Salt

**Mashed Potato Base:** *(reused in many dishes)*
- 2kg potatoes
- 1L lactose-free milk
- 300g butter
- 1 garlic clove
